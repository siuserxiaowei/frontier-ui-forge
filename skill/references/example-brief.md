# Example brief: Atlas incident console

## Product truth

Atlas helps a security analyst decide whether a suspicious sign-in needs containment within one minute. The primary job is triage, not reporting. Demo events are synthetic and must be labeled as such.

## Visitor mode

Operate. The first viewport must answer: what happened, how serious is it, what evidence supports that reading, and what can I do next?

## Three directions

1. Signal rail: dense left rail, evidence-first center, containment action anchored right. Monospaced numeric layer, cool graphite surface, one amber risk accent, short state transitions.
2. Case file: editorial split layout, large incident title, evidence timeline, paper-like light surface, serif display with neutral body, calm reveal motion.
3. Control room: wide operational grid, dark surface, compact data bands, cyan status system, direct keyboard-friendly actions, reduced motion by default.

## Selected contract

Select Signal rail when the analyst's time-to-decision matters more than narrative discovery. Keep the amber accent for risk only. Make the containment button change the evidence panel and audit trail immediately. The memorable moment is the risk signal resolving into a clear next action.

## Required states

Loading evidence, no matching events, expired session, failed enrichment, permission denied, containment pending, containment success, and containment failure with retry.

## Verification target

Check 390, 768, and 1280 widths, keyboard focus order, 4.5:1 text contrast, 44 by 44 touch targets, reduced motion, long usernames, and a 1000-event evidence list.
